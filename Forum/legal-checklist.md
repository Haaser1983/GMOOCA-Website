# Forum legal and launch checklist

Things GMOOCA must do or decide so that the protections the rules rely on actually apply. None of this is legal advice. Take this list and [rules.md](rules.md) to the NC attorney reviewing the corporate documents.

## Must do before registration opens

- [ ] **Register a DMCA designated agent** with the U.S. Copyright Office (online only: copyright.gov/dmca-directory). Required for the § 512(c) safe harbor that protects us from liability for members' infringing uploads.
  - Use the full legal name **GMOOCA, Inc.** and list alternate names: GMOOCA, Gaming Machine Owners, Operators and Collectors Association, gmooca.org.
  - Agent email: dmca@gmooca.org. A physical mailing address is required; a business address or PO box/mail service works if you don't want the home address published (check the current rules on PO boxes when filing).
  - **The designation expires after 3 years.** Put a renewal reminder on the calendar. A lapsed designation means no safe harbor.
  - Fee: a small per-filing fee (historically $6). Confirm when filing.
- [ ] **Publish Part 4 (Copyright and takedown policy)** as a page, with the same agent details as the registration.
- [ ] **Track repeat infringers** (three strikes in twelve months). The safe harbor requires a repeat-infringer policy that we actually enforce. Use an Invision warning reason called "Copyright" so strikes are countable.
- [ ] **Require agreement to the rules and terms at registration** (Invision registration terms checkbox). Keep the version and date accepted.
- [ ] **Set the minimum age to 18** in registration (Invision "minimum age" / date-of-birth requirement). This keeps us out of COPPA's under-13 rules and fits the gambling-adjacent subject matter.
- [ ] **Update the privacy policy** for forum data: accounts, IP logs, private messages, cookies set by Invision, payment data (handled by the processor), retention periods, and the account-deletion process.
- [ ] **Turn on spam protection**: registration CAPTCHA or question, email verification, new-member post moderation for the first N posts, link limits for new accounts.
- [ ] **Enable the Report button** for posts, messages, profiles and marketplace listings, and set who receives reports.

## Should do (strongly recommended)

- [ ] **Attorney review of Part 2 (Community terms)**, especially the liability cap, indemnity, venue clause and whether to add an arbitration clause or class-action waiver.
- [ ] **Decide marketplace scope.** The marketplace is the highest-risk area: interstate machine sales (Johnson Act), stolen goods, scams. Options: no marketplace at launch; parts-only first; machines only for supporting members. Have the attorney confirm that GMOOCA, by hosting listings only and not buying, selling or shipping, is not itself a "dealer" under 15 U.S.C. § 1173.
- [ ] **Download library policy in practice**: moderators approve uploads before they're public, at least at first. Reject manufacturer manuals, firmware and ROM images, PAR sheets and standards documents.
- [ ] **Business insurance**: ask about general liability plus media liability or cyber coverage that includes user-generated content claims (defamation, IP).
- [ ] **Keep moderation records**: who acted, what, when, why. Invision keeps a moderator log; don't purge it.
- [ ] **A law-enforcement and legal-request process**: who at GMOOCA receives subpoenas or preservation requests (compliance@), never respond off the cuff, and involve the attorney.
- [ ] **Separate staff and personal accounts**: GMOOCA official announcements come from staff accounts with a staff badge.

## Why these protections matter

| Protection | What it covers | What it doesn't |
|---|---|---|
| **Section 230** (47 U.S.C. § 230) | Generally shields GMOOCA from liability as the publisher of members' content (defamation, bad advice and so on), and protects good-faith removal of objectionable content. | Federal criminal law, intellectual property claims, certain sex-trafficking claims (FOSTA), and content GMOOCA itself creates or materially contributes to. Staff posts are GMOOCA's own content. |
| **DMCA § 512(c) safe harbor** | Copyright claims over member uploads, **if** we have a registered agent, act on valid notices, and enforce a repeat-infringer policy. | Anything we post ourselves, or anything we know is infringing and leave up. |
| **Terms of service** | The license to host content, disclaimers, liability cap, indemnity, venue, moderation discretion. | They don't override laws that can't be waived. Enforceability depends on clear assent (the registration checkbox). |

Practical consequences:
- **Staff should not post or "fix up" infringing material**, or write content that makes a member's illegal post our own. Remove it; don't edit it into shape.
- **Act promptly on DMCA notices** and keep the paperwork.
- **Staff posts and GMOOCA guides are GMOOCA's own speech**, with no Section 230 protection. Keep them accurate, sourced and free of legal advice.

## Calendar

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Before launch | Everything under "Must do" |
| Every year | Review the rules and terms; check that privacy policy retention matches practice |
| Every 3 years | Renew the DMCA agent designation |
| When adding paid tiers | Add billing, renewal and refund terms (Part 2 §12); update the privacy policy for payments |
| When opening the marketplace | Publish Part 3 and confirm the marketplace scope with the attorney |
