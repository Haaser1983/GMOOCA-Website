Quick Information

What is GMOOCA?
The Gaming Machine Owners Operators and Collectors Association is a dedicated community for small parlor operators, home users, and vintage machine collectors — focused on knowledge sharing, resources, and industry standards including SAS, G2S, and related protocols.
Why is the site down?
We're upgrading infrastructure, improving performance, and adding new features — including better desktop tool integration and community resources for machine management and standards discussion.
When will it be back?
Refer to the live countdown above. We aim for minimal downtime — typically complete within hours once started, though this longer window allows for thorough testing.
Resources in the meantime
Key industry documents covering SAS protocol implementation, G2S vs SAS comparison, SPC2/SPC2.5 setup guides, and GDS printer communication are available via our main site or direct industry sources (IGT, Aristocrat, IGSA/GSA).
