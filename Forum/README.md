# Forum

Everything for the GMOOCA Community forums (Invision Community at gmooca.org/Community). This folder is planning material. It is **not** part of the website build and isn't published.

| [legal-checklist.md](legal-checklist.md) | What we must do before launch (DMCA agent, age gate, terms acceptance…) and why it matters. |
| [moderation-guide.md](moderation-guide.md) | Internal guide for moderators: quick-reference actions, warning points, records. |
| [seed-content.md](seed-content.md) | Pinned posts and starter threads for launch day, plus the download library starter set. |
| [nlg-research.md](nlg-research.md) | Notes on New Life Games (NLG): structure, common questions, people to contact. |

Related on the website: `src/Community/` (the placeholder page with the short ground rules) and `src/_data/forums.json` (planned sections). When the full rules are final, update the five short rules on the community page to match Part 1.
