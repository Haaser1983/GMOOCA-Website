Resources & Knowledge Base

GMOOCA is building a comprehensive, community-curated library of gaming machine standards, hardware guides, and technical documents. These resources help operators maintain efficient floors and collectors preserve historical machines — all discussed and expanded in the forums.

SAS (Slot Accounting System)

IGT's de facto standard for meter reporting, event logging, player tracking, bonusing, ticketing, and cashless gaming. Widely used for interoperability in casinos and smaller operations.

View Excerpts & Diagrams
G2S (Game to System)

Advanced protocol with multi-host support, remote configuration, meter subscriptions, and server-based bonusing. Superior scalability compared to SAS in modern setups.

View Comparison Table
Aristocrat SPC Converters (SPC2 / SPC2.5)

Serial Protocol Converters for bridging legacy and modern systems — setup guides, LED diagnostics, RAM clear procedures, and port layouts.

View Setup & Board Images
GDS Printer Protocol

Gaming Standards Association's USB HID class device standard for printers in gaming environments (v1.0, 2006).
