# Part 1: Community rules

GMOOCA is a community for people who own, run and keep gaming machines. These rules exist to keep it useful, friendly and on the right side of the law. By joining, you agree to them and to the Community terms in Part 2.

## 1. Be useful and be decent
- Treat other members with respect, whether they've owned one machine for a week or run a shop for thirty years.
- Criticize ideas, not people. No insults, name-calling, threats, harassment or pile-ons.
- No discrimination or hateful content based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age or disability.
- Keep it clean enough for a public workshop. No sexual or graphic content.
- Answer the question that was asked. If you don't know, say so.

## 2. Stay on topic, post in the right place
- Post in the section for your machine or subject, and search before you start a new thread.
- Use a clear title ("IGT S2000 – blank VFD after battery change", not "HELP!!!").
- Don't post the same thing in several sections, hijack someone else's thread, or bump threads just to bump them.
- Mark your thread **[SOLVED]** and say what fixed it.

## 3. No legal advice
Gaming machine laws vary widely by state and change over time. You may share your own experience and link to official sources, but no one here, including GMOOCA staff, gives legal advice, and nothing posted here is legal advice. Don't tell others an activity is legal for them. Talk to an attorney about your situation.

## 4. No cheating, no security bypasses
Don't post or ask for help with:
- defeating or bypassing a machine's locks, seals, security chips, software authentication, licensing, or accounting and meters;
- cheating devices or methods (bill validator or coin spoofing, counterfeit or altered tickets, "hack shop" chips, manipulating payouts on a machine you don't own);
- tampering with any machine on a casino or other commercial floor, or with any machine you don't own;
- concealing the origin of a machine (removing serial plates or asset tags, altering records).

Configuring and repairing **your own** machine, the way its manufacturer allows, is fine. That includes setup menus, RAM clears, routine repairs, and talking about how a feature works. If in doubt, ask a moderator first.

## 5. Share only what's yours to share
- **Allowed:** your own words, photos and drawings, your own original software and designs, and links to official or freely published sources.
- **Not allowed:** copies of manufacturer manuals, service bulletins, schematics, firmware, game software or ROM/EPROM/CF images, PAR sheets or other confidential documents, paid or licensed standards, or anything you don't have the right to share. Don't request or offer them in public or in private messages.
- Asking where to buy a manual legitimately, or who sells a part, is fine.
- Credit people and sources. If you quote, keep it short and link the original.

## 6. No gambling and no gambling promotion
- No real-money wagering, betting pools or games of chance run through the community.
- No promoting or linking to online casinos, sportsbooks, sweepstakes or "skill game" operations, prediction markets, or gambling affiliate sites.
- No recruiting for or advertising illegal gambling operations of any kind.

## 7. Safety first
Gaming machines contain mains voltage, high-voltage monitor circuits, heavy moving parts and cabinets that can tip. Advice here is shared by volunteers and may be wrong for your machine. Unplug before you work, follow the manufacturer's safety instructions, and call a qualified technician when you're not sure. **You work on your equipment at your own risk.**

## 8. Respect privacy
- Don't post anyone's personal information (home address, phone number, private emails or messages, workplace details, photos of people without their consent) unless it's your own.
- Don't post casino or route-operator employee details, surveillance information, or asset numbers that identify a venue's machines.
- Private messages are private. Don't publish them without the sender's consent, except when you report abuse to moderators.

## 9. Be honest about who you are
- One account per person. No impersonating others, including GMOOCA staff, manufacturers or other members.
- If you work for, own or are paid by a company whose products you discuss (a parts vendor, a distributor, a software maker, even a competitor of GMOOCA), say so in your profile and when you recommend its products.
- No spam: no unsolicited ads, link-dropping, referral codes or SEO posts. Commercial listings go only in the marketplace, under Part 3.

## 10. AI-generated content
If you use an AI tool to write an answer or a guide, say so and check it before you post. Wrong technical advice can damage machines and hurt people. Don't post AI output you haven't verified.

## 11. Members must be 18 or older
You must be at least 18 to join.

## 12. Moderators have the final say
Moderators may edit, move, lock or remove content and restrict accounts to keep the community within these rules, sometimes without notice. If you disagree with a decision, appeal privately (see Part 5). Don't argue about it in public threads.

# Part 3: Marketplace rules

The marketplace (classifieds) lets members buy, sell and trade machines, parts and related items. **GMOOCA is not a party to any transaction.** We don't own, inspect, hold, ship, guarantee or insure anything listed, and we don't process payments between members.

## Who can list
- Members in good standing who meet the posting requirements set by moderators (for example a minimum account age or post count, or supporting membership). Moderators may adjust these to reduce scams.
- Businesses may list only with a clearly marked business account or profile.

## What you can't list
- Anything illegal to sell or ship to the buyer's location.
- Stolen goods, or machines with removed, altered or covered serial plates or identification.
- Machines or parts still owned by or tagged to a casino, route or other operator, unless they were legitimately released for sale.
- Cheating devices, counterfeit tickets or ticket stock made to imitate real tickets, security-bypass chips or tools, or anything built to defeat a machine's security, licensing or accounting.
- Copies of game software, firmware, ROM/EPROM/CF images, manuals or other copyrighted material, unless you are the rights holder. Original, legitimately owned media and parts are fine.
- Currency, gift cards, accounts or credits for gambling sites, or any gambling service.
- Weapons, drugs, alcohol, tobacco and anything unrelated to gaming machines, coin-op, arcade or game-room equipment.

## Listing requirements
- Describe the item honestly: condition, what's included (keys, validator, printer, media), whether it powers up and plays, known faults, and the item's location.
- Use your own photos of the actual item.
- State the price or "best offer", accepted payment methods and shipping or pickup terms.
- Mark listings sold or withdrawn promptly.

## Legal compliance is the buyer's and seller's job
- **Buyers and sellers are solely responsible** for complying with all laws that apply to the sale, purchase, possession and transport of gaming machines and related devices. That includes state ownership laws, which often differ by a machine's age or type, and federal law on transporting gambling devices across state lines (the Johnson Act, 15 U.S.C. §§ 1171–1178, which also sets registration and record-keeping duties for businesses that deal in these devices).
- Sellers should not ship a machine to a place where the buyer can't lawfully own it. Buyers should confirm their state's rules before buying.
- GMOOCA does not give legal advice about any transaction.

## Safety and scams
- Use payment methods with buyer and seller protection. Avoid wire transfers, gift cards and "friends and family" payments with people you don't know.
- Inspect large items in person or by live video before paying when you can. Meet in public places for local pickups.
- Report suspected scams to moderators right away. We may remove listings and restrict accounts, and we may share information with law enforcement as described in Part 2, section 14.
- Disputes between members are between those members. Moderators may step in to keep threads civil, but don't arbitrate refunds or returns.

# Part 4: Copyright and takedown policy

GMOOCA respects intellectual property rights and expects members to do the same. We respond to notices of alleged infringement under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512).

## Reporting infringement
Send a written notice to our designated agent:

> **Designated agent:** GMOOCA, Inc., Attn: Copyright Agent
> **Email:** [dmca@gmooca.org](mailto:dmca@gmooca.org)
> **Mail:** *[business mailing address as registered with the U.S. Copyright Office]*

Your notice must include:
1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed;
3. identification of the material you claim is infringing, with enough information to locate it (for example the URL);
4. your name, address, telephone number and email address;
5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law; and
6. a statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

We remove or disable access to material identified in a valid notice and notify the member who posted it.

## Counter-notification
If your material was removed and you believe that was a mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the same agent including:
1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. identification of the material removed and where it appeared before removal;
3. a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification;
4. your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if you're outside the U.S., any judicial district in which GMOOCA may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice or their agent.

We forward valid counter-notices to the original complainant. We may restore the material in 10 to 14 business days unless the complainant tells us they have filed a court action.

## Repeat infringers
We terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of members who repeatedly infringe. Under our policy, a member who receives three valid copyright strikes within twelve months has their account closed. We may close an account sooner for serious or willful infringement.

## Misrepresentation
Anyone who knowingly makes a material misrepresentation in a notice or counter-notice may be liable for damages under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

## Other intellectual property
For trademark, trade secret or other rights complaints, write to [dmca@gmooca.org](mailto:dmca@gmooca.org) with the same detail. We review these case by case.

# Part 5: Enforcement

## How moderation works
Most problems are handled with a friendly note. When that doesn't work, we use a warning-points system:

| Level | Typical cause | Typical action |
|---|---|---|
| Note | First minor slip (wrong section, missing details) | Content moved or edited, private note |
| Warning (1 point) | Repeated minor issues, mild incivility, unapproved promotion | Formal warning; points expire after 6 months |
| Moderated (3 points) | Continued problems after warnings | New posts need approval for 30 days |
| Suspension (5 points) | Harassment, serious or repeated violations | Account suspended for 7–30 days |
| Ban | Threats, doxxing, scams, cheating or bypass content, illegal content, ban evasion, repeat infringement | Permanent removal |

Serious violations can skip straight to suspension or a ban. Moderators may take any action needed to protect members, GMOOCA or the public.

## Reporting
Use the **Report** button on any post, message or listing. Reports go only to moderators. Don't argue with or call out a rule-breaker in public; report and move on.

## Appeals
If you think a moderation decision was wrong, email [compliance@gmooca.org](mailto:compliance@gmooca.org) within 30 days with your username, the content or action involved, and why. A different staff member reviews it, usually within 14 days, and that decision is final. Copyright removals follow the counter-notice process in Part 4 instead.

## Staff
Staff and moderators are identified by their group badge. Moderators are volunteers or GMOOCA staff. Their posts are their own unless they're marked as official GMOOCA announcements.
