About GMOOCA

The Gaming Machine Owners, Operators, and Collectors Association (GMOOCA) is a professional community dedicated to supporting small gambling parlor operators, home users, and passionate collectors who own, maintain, or restore gaming equipment.

Our Mission

To foster a trustworthy, accessible hub for sharing technical knowledge on industry standards (SAS, G2S, GDS), hardware troubleshooting (SPC converters, printers), and best practices for machine management and restoration.

We prioritize security, accessibility, and community respect — delivering a professional environment that celebrates the technical and historical value of gaming machines without gaudy aesthetics.

Our Vision

To build a long-term, scalable platform integrated with Invision Community, featuring single sign-on, subscription-based licensing, desktop-web bridges, and community-driven resources that empower operators and collectors for years to come.

Who We Serve

  • Small Parlor Operators: Practical tools and discussions for floor optimization, compliance, and maintenance.
  • Home Users & Collectors: Restoration guides, protocol explanations, and a supportive network for vintage and modern machines.
  • Enthusiasts & Professionals: A tasteful, confident space to connect, learn, and advance the hobby/profession.
